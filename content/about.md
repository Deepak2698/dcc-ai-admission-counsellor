## About the institute
Starlight Coaching Centre is a coaching institute for JEE, NEET and school foundation (classes 8 to 10). Classes run in small batches of up to 30 students, with weekly tests, doubt-clearing sessions and parent meetings every month. Page: /courses

## Admission process
1. Book a free counselling session or demo class. 2. Take the free scholarship and placement test (60 minutes, online or at the centre). 3. Choose a batch and pay the registration fee of ₹2,000, which is adjusted in the course fee. Admissions are open all year; new batches start every April and July. Page: /admissions

## Scholarships
Scholarships of 10% to 50% on tuition are given on the scholarship test score. Students who scored 90% or above in their last school exam get an extra 10%. Siblings of current students get 5% off. Scholarships cannot be combined beyond 60% in total. Page: /admissions

## Refund policy
Full refund within 7 days of the first class. After that, 50% of the remaining fee is refunded within 30 days of joining. No refund after 30 days, but a student can move to a later batch once. Registration fee is not refundable.
