# Courses

## JEE Main + Advanced course (2-year programme)
Our main JEE course for students entering class 11. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with JEE Main and Advanced level problem solving. Duration: 2 years, 6 days a week, 4 hours a day. Fee: ₹1,40,000 per year (₹1,25,000 if paid in full). Includes printed study material, 40 full-length mock tests and the online test series. Page: /courses/jee

## JEE Main crash course
For class 12 and repeaters. 4 months of revision and 25 full-length mock tests, starting every December. Fee: ₹35,000. Page: /courses/jee-crash

## NEET course (2-year programme)
Our main NEET course for students entering class 11. Physics, Chemistry and Biology with NCERT-focused teaching and NEET-level tests. Duration: 2 years, 6 days a week, 4 hours a day. Fee: ₹1,30,000 per year (₹1,15,000 if paid in full). Includes study material, 40 mock tests and weekly biology diagrams practice. Page: /courses/neet

## NEET repeater batch
For students who have appeared for NEET once. 10 months, daily tests and personal mentoring. Fee: ₹1,10,000. Page: /courses/neet-repeater

## Foundation (classes 8 to 10)
Maths and Science for school exams, Olympiads and NTSE, building the base for JEE and NEET. 3 days a week, 2 hours a day, evenings after school. Fee: ₹45,000 per year. Page: /courses/foundation

## Instalments
Course fees can be paid in 3 instalments: 40% at admission, 30% after 3 months and 30% after 6 months. EMI options with 0% interest are available for 6 or 9 months through partner banks.
