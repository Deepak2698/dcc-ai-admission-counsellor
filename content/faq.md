# Frequently asked questions

## Batch timings
Morning batch 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, afternoon batch 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM (for droppers and repeaters), evening batch 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM for school students. Foundation classes are 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Faculty
All subjects are taught by full-time faculty with 8 to 20 years of JEE and NEET teaching experience. Each batch has a dedicated mentor who tracks test scores and talks to parents every month.

## Results
Every year we publish our results with student names and ranks on the results page, with written consent from the students.

## Online classes
All courses are also available online with live classes, recordings for 7 days and the same tests. Online fees are 30% lower than classroom fees.

## Hostel and transport
We do not run a hostel, but we share a list of verified hostels and PGs near the centre. School bus pick-up is available on 3 routes for an extra ₹1,500 per month.

## Doubt sessions
Doubt-clearing sessions run every day for an hour after class, and students can post doubts in the app any time; faculty reply within 24 hours.
